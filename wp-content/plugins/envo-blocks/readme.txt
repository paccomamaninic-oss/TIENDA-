== Description ==

Envo Blocks gives the block editor ready-made store pages, store layouts and the WooCommerce blocks and features that WordPress and WooCommerce do not have – no page builder needed.

= Store in 5 minutes =

A setup wizard (Envo Blocks › Store in 5 minutes) builds the whole store for you: pick the theme (our free Envo Blocks Store is recommended, any block theme works), one of the templates and your brand colour – with a live preview of every page – and the wizard creates the pages with your own products, sets the front page, the header and footer, your store name or logo and the colours of the whole site. An optional "Your store" step sets the country, currency, payment and shipping basics and the store pages, and can add a few sample products. "Undo everything" puts the site back as it was.

= Templates, sections and presets =

* **Templates Import** in the editor: [12 free templates](https://demos.envoblocks.com/template-demos/) with their pages (home, About us, Help, Contact…), 123 page sections and 140 block presets.
* One-click import with or without the header and footer; required plugins and the recommended theme are shown with their status.
* Only the layout, texts and settings are imported – the blocks show the products of your own store.
* Our free [Envo Blocks Store](https://wordpress.org/themes/envo-blocks-store/) theme installs and activates from WordPress.org right in Templates Import, with one click.

= Store Layouts =

Give the product page, shop and categories, cart, checkout, order received page, My account and the side cart your own design, made of blocks – in block and classic themes. Start from 33 free designs in several colours (see the [layout demos](https://demos.envoblocks.com/layout-demos/)), change anything in the editor, and the theme header and footer stay. The checkout can be distraction-free: your logo, a secure checkout note and a link back to the cart instead of the menu. The side cart slides in after Add to cart with the cart items, the free shipping progress and the totals.

= Wishlist =

A heart button on product pages and in product lists saves products for later – for guests (in the browser) and for customers (in their account, on every device). The wishlist page lists them with price, stock and Add to cart. Choose the page in WooCommerce settings, and the look of the button and the list in the blocks.

= PDF invoices =

Invoices, credit notes and packing slips for WooCommerce orders, made on your site:

* presets for 18 countries with the right words, tax IDs and tax texts – and editable labels for any other country;
* numbers without gaps (yearly, monthly or one series), documents that never change once issued, credit notes for refunds and "Cancel and reissue";
* the PDF in the order e-mails, a download button in My account and the Invoice Download block, bulk issuing in the orders;
* the tax summary per rate, the total in words, a payment QR code on unpaid bank transfer invoices, right-to-left documents and font packs for Japanese, Chinese, Korean, Indian scripts, Arabic, Hebrew and Thai.

= Product filters =

The Product Filter and Active Filters blocks filter the shop, the categories and the search by price (slider), category, attribute (color, size…), rating and stock, with the number of products of every option. Chosen filters show as chips with "Clear all". Filtered lists have their own address, so they can be shared.

= More store features =

* **Lowest price (EU Omnibus)** – the price history of every product and variation and "Lowest price in the last 30 days" under discounted prices.
* **Prices with or without tax** – the Tax Switcher block lets visitors switch every price of the store between with and without VAT (when WooCommerce calculates taxes).
* **Checkout Fields** – show, hide, rename, reorder and make required the checkout fields and add your own text, select and checkbox fields, for the block and the classic checkout.
* **Product Tabs** – Description, Reviews, your own tabs and the tabs of other plugins in any order, as tabs or an accordion, plus own tabs for each product.
* **Product videos** – YouTube, Vimeo, Dailymotion or your own video in the product gallery; the player loads only after a click.
* **Product questions** – customers ask on the product page, you answer in the admin, the answer goes out by e-mail and shows for everyone.
* **Review photos** – customers add photos to their reviews (saved as small WebP without GPS data); the Customer Photos block shows them as a gallery.
* **Catalog mode** – hide prices and Add to cart for everyone, for visitors who are not signed in, or in chosen categories.
* **Product feed** – one XML feed for Google Merchant Center and the Meta catalog with prices, stock, images and variations, updated every hour.
* **FAQ** – questions that open and close, with FAQPage data for search engines.
* **Form Styler** – a form of Contact Form 7, WPForms, Fluent Forms, Ninja Forms, Formidable Forms, Forminator or Gravity Forms in the look of your site: field style, two columns, the button and your colors.
* **Payment QR Code** – a QR code for bank transfer orders (SPD for CZK, EPC / SEPA for EUR) on the thank-you page and in the e-mails.
* **Entrance animations** for any block, also the blocks of WordPress, WooCommerce and other plugins.

= Blocks =

Product Filter, Active Filters, Product Tabs, FAQ, Form Styler, Language Switcher, Product Questions, Customer Photos, Lowest Price, Tax Switcher, Wishlist, Free Shipping Progress, Stock Indicator, Delivery Estimate, Trust Badges, Features Bar, Coupon Code Box, Rating Summary, Recently Viewed Products, Order Tracking, Size Guide, Payment QR Code, Invoice Download, Pricing Table, Advanced Heading, Parallax Section, Sticky Column, Announcement Bar, Countdown, Opening Hours, Map, Video Popup and the blocks of the store layouts. * Every block has a **Documentation** button in its settings that opens its guide, in your language where there is one.

== Installation ==

1. Install the plugin from Plugins > Add New (or upload the `envo-blocks` folder to `/wp-content/plugins/`) and activate it.
2. Open a page in the block editor: use the **Templates Import** button in the header, or find the blocks in the **Envo Blocks** section of the inserter.
3. Or let **Envo Blocks > Store in 5 minutes** build the whole store from a template in your brand colour.
4. The store features are in **Envo Blocks > Settings**.

= Does the plugin need WooCommerce? =

The templates, sections, presets and the content blocks (FAQ, Form Styler, Pricing Table, Advanced Heading, Trust Badges, Map, Video Popup…) work without it. The store blocks and store features need WooCommerce.

= Does the plugin connect to external services? =

Only in the cases listed under "External services" below, and only when you or your visitors use them. Everything else works on your own site.

= Can the map load only after the visitor agrees? =

Yes. Turn on "Load the map only after a click" in the Privacy settings of the Map block. Until the visitor clicks, nothing is loaded from the map service.

= Are the payment icons official logos? =

No. They are simplified marks drawn for this plugin so that visitors recognise the payment methods. All trademarks belong to their owners.

= My theme has no canvas template. Can I still import the templates? =

Yes. Envo Blocks adds the page template **Envo Blocks Canvas (no header and footer)** when the theme has none. It keeps the styles of your theme.

= Does it work on multilingual sites? =

Yes, with WPML, Polylang and TranslatePress: the texts of the blocks and settings can be translated, store layouts and rules work in every language and search, quick view and other parts that load later come in the language of the page. The Language Switcher block shows their languages in your header or footer. See the guide "Multilingual sites".

= Can I translate the plugin? =

Yes. Translations are managed on translate.wordpress.org; a POT file is in the `languages` folder.

== External services ==

The plugin connects to these services only in the cases below. Each request carries only what is listed, plus what any web request sends (IP address, user agent).

* **Google Maps** (Google LLC) or **OpenStreetMap** (OpenStreetMap Foundation) – the Map block loads the map in the visitor's browser when the page is shown, or after a click if you choose so. Sent: the place, zoom and language set in the block. Google: [Terms](https://policies.google.com/terms), [Maps terms](https://maps.google.com/help/terms_maps/), [Privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). OpenStreetMap: [Terms](https://wiki.osmfoundation.org/wiki/Terms_of_Use), [Tile policy](https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/tiles/), [Privacy](https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy).
* **YouTube** (Google LLC, youtube-nocookie.com), **Vimeo** (Vimeo.com, Inc.) or **Dailymotion** (Dailymotion SA) – the Video Popup block and product videos load the player only after the visitor clicks play. Sent: the video ID and start time. YouTube: [Terms](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms), [Privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Vimeo: [Terms](https://vimeo.com/terms), [Privacy](https://vimeo.com/privacy). Dailymotion: [Terms](https://legal.dailymotion.com/en/terms-of-use/), [Privacy](https://legal.dailymotion.com/en/privacy-policy/).
* **assets.envoblocks.com** (Envo Blocks, by EnvoThemes) – preview images in the template window (only those you scroll to, saved on your site after the first view) and the PDF invoice font packs (only when you click "Download" in Envo Blocks > Invoices > Fonts; checked against a checksum and stored on your site). Sent: only the file address. [Terms](https://envoblocks.com/terms/), [Privacy](https://envoblocks.com/privacy-policy/).
* **Template library** – assets.envoblocks.com/library/ (backup: envo-demos.com/library/) and the preview list assets.envoblocks.com/previews/manifest.json (Envo Blocks, by EnvoThemes): twice a day, only in the admin and in WP-Cron, the plugin loads the public list of the library, so new free templates, sections, presets and store layouts appear without a plugin update (and a notice tells you about new templates); the file of such a design is downloaded only when you import or insert it, and checked against a checksum. Sent: the plugin version in the user agent and the file address. Turn it off with the filter `envo_blocks_remote_library`. [Terms](https://envoblocks.com/terms/), [Privacy](https://envoblocks.com/privacy-policy/).
* **demos.envoblocks.com** (Envo Blocks, by EnvoThemes) – the images of a template, section or preset you import or insert, or of the template you choose in Store in 5 minutes, are downloaded once into your Media Library. Sent: only the image address. [Terms](https://envoblocks.com/terms/), [Privacy](https://envoblocks.com/privacy-policy/).
* **WordPress.org** – when you click "Install & activate" for a plugin or theme a template needs (or for the Envo Blocks Store theme), WordPress installs it like Plugins > Add New. In the admin only, while that theme is not installed, Templates Import and the setup notice ask the WordPress.org themes API whether it is available (at most once a day) and show its screenshot from ts.w.org. Sent: the plugin or theme slug and the usual data of a WordPress update request. [Terms](https://wordpress.org/about/terms-of-service/), [Privacy](https://wordpress.org/about/privacy/).
